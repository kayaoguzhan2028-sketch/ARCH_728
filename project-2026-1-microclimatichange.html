This microclimatic mapping deconstructs the relationship between industrial heat emissions and regional ecological displacement. The pink gradient represents the elevated surface and atmospheric temperatures enveloping the power plant site, which alter the local boundary layer climate. Migratory birds traveling through the Nallıhan basin rely heavily on predictable thermal and atmospheric currents. When intersecting with the anthropocentric heat plume of Çayırhan, the historic skyway is fractured, forcing species into an energy-intensive, deviated path to circumvent the hazardous microclimatic zone.
