Beytepe pond is located near the left corner of the selected site. It is relatively higher to the rest. There is definitive information that this is artificial due to the need of irrigation at first. Then, it was used for nourishment of endangered fish species and research on zooplankton populations was made. These events have served for the creation of the pond's microclimate. Although its relationship with the botanical garden still remains, due to technical difficulties the pathway towards the pond has been closed (flood risk of the pedestrian way).
