The rendered view shows the new residential blocks as they present themselves: solid, complete, grounded. Nothing in the finished form suggests what it replaced. Slide to see what the foundations are made of. The gecekondu fabric emerges beneath the concrete, not cleared but compressed, incorporated into the very structure that displaced it. The rubble of one form of habitation has become the ground condition of another. In this case, the debris of the "gecekondu era" are not buried, but dissolve and transform into a new form as the new urban soil.
