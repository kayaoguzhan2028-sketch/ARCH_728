From the 1930s onwards, İmrahor's clay-rich soils supported a thriving brick industry. While early production in the 1930s relied on traditional methods, brick production expanded significantly between 1965 and 1976 with the establishment of 17 factories¹. By the early 1970s, the valley contained approximately 25 brick kilns² and had become one of Ankara's major brick-producing areas, supplying materials for the city's rapid growth. Although most factories closed by the early 2000s and the last remaining factory ceased operation in early 2020s, the traces of production remain visible in the landscape through abandoned structures, pits, and bricks embedded in the soil. In the visual, we see the factories that we can locate today.
