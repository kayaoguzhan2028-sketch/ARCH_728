This intervention is rooted in Bruno Latour's concept of Living with Frankenstein. Rather than erasing our industrial and technological creations (landfills, mine pits, oil spills), we accept them as legitimate elements of urban design worthy of attention and respect. The sifter evaluates these scenes through a critical matrix of dualities to determine the required care. These diagnostic questions map across multi-dimensional matrices, evaluating layers from "waste contamination" to "environment & nature," and from "trash" and "residues" to "humans & non-humans".

CARE OR NOT-CARE?, IN ATTENTION OR ABANDONED?, INTERNAL OR EXTERNAL?,
LOVE OR HATE?, WORTHY OR UN-WORTHY?, OPPORTUNITY OR THREAT?,
WANTED OR UNWANTED?, POTENTIAL OR ERROR?, NOT-SIFTED OR SIFTED?
