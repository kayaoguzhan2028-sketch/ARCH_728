Urban Soil Metabolism · Spring Semester · 8 Project

Soil is examined as a metabolically active urban medium — continuously produced, displaced, transformed, and regulated through intertwined human and non-human forces. In this context, Ankara foregrounds the soil's agency, revealing the ground as a dynamic force within the city's transformations.

"When does soil become visible — and when is it rendered invisible in the city?"

— Visibility

"When is soil alive, inert, or waste? And whose labor sustains it?"

— (In)Habitability

"What does soil carry and transform as it moves?"

— Transportability
