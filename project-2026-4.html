Beytepe Hospital construction was between 2013 and 2016. Provided superposed aerial view proves that there is significant soil exchange between 2007 and 2023 models. As the 2023 shows higher topographical levels, it is evident that the excavated soil is laid around in order to flatten the area.

This section goes right through the road in diagonal from the hospital to Beytepe pond. That is why the section line differs very little in height. However, the 2007 section reveals a great deal of soil removement. The reason of alteration is the excavation/refill processes during the construction process of the hospital. This section is an example that reflects soil movement in excavation. In the 3D model the soil movement can be perceived better.

Beytepe pond is located near the left corner of the selected site. It is relatively higher to the rest. There is definitive information that this is artificial due to the need of irrigation at first. Then, it was used for nourishment of endangered fish species and research on zooplankton populations was made. These events have served for the creation of the pond's microclimate. Although its relationship with the botanical garden still remains, due to technical difficulties the pathway towards the pond has been closed (flood risk of the pedestrian way).

The arboretum is located at the entrance of the Botanical Garden. The Botanical Garden is composed of three parts: the first part is the entrance, the second part is the ecological bridge, the third part is the seed museum and related buildings. Lastly, there is its connection to the pond, which is closed due to security reasons. The buildings are not fully complete due to budget reasons, therefore the arboretum part is now incomplete, which resulted in wilderness and the occurrence of several species that can be observed everywhere. Some of these species are not seen at the other sites; however, since there's no interruption of parts, this place has a diverse quality of new and existing species compared to the other parts, and this section is a crucial example of how incompleteness and being left alone can offer ecological refurbishment.

This section is directly connected to the 6th one, because they examine the same area from different perspectives. This subjected road is the source of inspiration of this study as it demonstrates such qualities as vegetation, level-based complexity, retaining walls and adjacency to water infrastructure, which are given specific attention. How the pile foundation relates with the underneath soil formation and the soil spread around the road's infrastructure is worth examining. The road here consists of several levels and entangled routes from Beysukent to Bilkent. Diversity of the routes is significant because the levels create hollows for vegetation. The soil in concern is the subject of excavation of the hospital site, yet there are traces of the reeds and more.
